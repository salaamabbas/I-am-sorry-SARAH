My Sarah

The love of my entire life

I love you so much

Sarah… I’ve never been more sorry in my life.

I hurt the one person who makes every single day worth living. These past few days without you have been the emptiest, darkest days I’ve ever known. You are my light, my home, my whole world — and I was stupid enough to make you doubt that.

Last night I couldn’t sleep, so I drove to the pier where we watched the sunrise on your birthday last year… the one where you cried happy tears because I surprised you with coffee and that tiny cake. I sat on the exact same bench, staring at the empty spot next to me where you should have been. I actually broke down crying like a child because I realized I might never feel your head on my shoulder there again. That moment is supposed to be ours forever, and I almost ruined it.

I miss your voice, your laugh, the way you fit perfectly in my arms. I took it all for granted and I hate myself for it.

My promise to you

I swear on everything I am:

• I will listen with my whole heart
• I will choose you every single day
• Pride will never come between us again
• I will hold you tighter and love you louder
• I will spend forever making sure you feel as cherished as you truly are

You are my forever. Please, baby… forgive me.
